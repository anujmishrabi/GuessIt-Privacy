# GuessIt Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 2026**

GuessIt ("the App") is a car identification game developed by Anuj Mishra.

## Information We Collect

GuessIt does not collect, transmit, or share personal information.

The App does not require you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or other personal information.

## Data Stored on Your Device

GuessIt stores certain gameplay information locally on your device using local device storage.

This may include:

- Games played
- Scores
- Correct answers
- Best streaks
- Achievements
- Cars identified
- Daily Challenge completion status
- Daily Challenge scores and streaks

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to the developer or to third parties by GuessIt.

## Analytics and Advertising

GuessIt does not currently use third-party analytics services or advertising services.

## Data Sharing

GuessIt does not sell, share, or transmit your personal information to third parties.

## External Links

The Credits section of GuessIt contains links to Wikimedia Commons and Creative Commons websites to provide image sources and license information.

When you open an external link, you leave GuessIt and the linked website's own privacy practices apply.

## Children's Privacy

GuessIt does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Because the App does not require an account or collect personal information, it can be used without providing personal information.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the App's functionality or data practices change.

The latest version will be made available at the published Privacy Policy URL.
